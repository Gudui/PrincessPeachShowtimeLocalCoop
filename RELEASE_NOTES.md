# v0.1.0-alpha.1 release notes

Welcome to the initial alpha release of **Princess Peach Showtime Local Co-op**!

## Highlights

- **Native 2-Player Local Co-op:** Play through Princess Peach: Showtime! together on a single screen with two separate controllers.
- **Dynamic Dual-Player Shared Camera:** Smoothly frames both players and widens to accommodate player separation.
- **Independent Health & Life Gauges:** Both players have isolated damage, recovery, and UI gauge displays.
- **Catch-up Leash:** Keeps players together during exploration, automatically bringing Player 2 back when navigating long stages.
- **Customizable Appearance:** Player 2 sports a distinctive cyan emissive tint, fully configurable in `romfs/PShowCoop/settings.ini`.
- **Easy Installation:** Automated installer script for Ryujinx and Atmosphere SD card deployments.
